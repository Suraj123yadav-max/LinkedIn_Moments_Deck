# LinkedIn Year-Round Engagement Product Strategy

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/)

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Product Strategy and User Research Case Study for LinkedIn**, focused on increasing **year-round engagement among students and early-career professionals**.

The objective was to understand why LinkedIn usage drops after users secure internships or jobs, identify key user pain points, analyze the competitive landscape, and propose actionable product solutions to encourage meaningful professional engagement beyond job searching.

The deck demonstrates structured product thinking by combining **user research, competitive analysis, user personas, journey mapping, prioritization frameworks, product strategy, solution design, and success metrics**.

---

## Objectives

- Understand LinkedIn's current product ecosystem and engagement model.
- Analyze LinkedIn against competing social platforms such as Instagram and Snapchat.
- Identify user pain points through qualitative user research.
- Understand user behavior before and after securing job opportunities.
- Develop user personas and map the customer journey.
- Prioritize key pain points using Reach × Impact analysis.
- Propose product solutions to increase meaningful contribution.

## Business Problems Addressed

### 1. Understanding the Engagement Gap

**Objective:** Understand why LinkedIn is often treated as a career utility rather than a daily professional habit.

The analysis identified that users primarily engage with LinkedIn for job hunting, networking, and career-related activities, while engagement decreases after immediate career goals are achieved.

---

### 2. Identifying User Pain Points

**Objective:** Understand the key barriers preventing students and early-career professionals from engaging regularly.

Key pain points include:

- Achievement posts receive more visibility than useful content.
- Notifications are flooded with work anniversaries and job changes.
- AI-generated content has reduced trust in the feed.
- Users feel pressure to post something "impressive."
- LinkedIn provides limited reasons to return after securing a job.

---

### 3. User Research & Validation

**Objective:** Validate the engagement problem through qualitative user research.

**9 users were interviewed**, revealing:

- **7/9** users mentioned reduced usage after getting an offer.
- **4/9** users felt LinkedIn is more about showcasing success.
- **6/9** users felt the feed contains self-promotion instead of useful content.
- Only **2/9** users found LinkedIn meaningful for building long-term professional value.

---

### 4. Competitive Benchmarking

**Objective:** Compare LinkedIn with platforms such as Instagram and Snapchat across engagement behavior.

The analysis considered:

- Primary purpose
- Usage frequency
- Average session duration
- Content creation
- Engagement style

Key insight: LinkedIn is treated more as a **transactional utility**, while competing platforms have stronger engagement loops that encourage frequent return behavior.

---

### 5. User Personas & Journey Mapping

**Objective:** Understand user motivations, goals, behaviors, and friction points.

The case study developed personas including:

**Atishya Jain — 23, Product Analyst**
- Wants to build his professional network, track career progress, and stay visible to recruiters.
- Faces difficulty finding useful content and pressure to share impressive achievements.

**Yukti Dahiya — 24, Software Engineer**
- Wants to share technical knowledge, build domain credibility, and support early-career professionals.
- Faces achievement-heavy content, notification noise, and re-engagement friction.

The journey mapping highlighted a cycle of **passive consumption → irrelevant content → notification noise → hesitation to share → session exit**.

---

### 6. Pain Point Prioritization

**Objective:** Prioritize problems based on potential reach and impact.

Pain points were evaluated using a **Reach × Impact scoring approach derived from the RICE framework**.

The highest-priority problem was:

> **Sharing feels forced due to pressure to post something "impressive."**

**Reach: 9/10 | Impact: 9/10 | Score: 81/100**

---

### 7. Product Recommendations

**Objective:** Design customer-centric solutions addressing the highest-priority problems.

### LinkedIn Moments

A proposed **24-hour disappearing sharing format** designed to make professional sharing more casual and low-pressure.

Key features:

- 24-hour disappearing content
- Visible to 1st-degree connections
- Text, photo, and voice sharing
- Low-pressure interaction
- Encourages authentic professional updates

**Goal:** Reduce the "nothing big enough to post" barrier and encourage organic sharing.

### Community Badges

A proposed **peer-validated recognition system** that rewards meaningful contributions rather than simply rewarding visibility.

Contribution loop:

`Contribute → Get Verified → Earn Badge → Build Reputation`

**Goal:** Move users from passive scrolling toward meaningful contribution and build authentic professional credibility.

---

### 8. Feature Prioritization

**Objective:** Prioritize product initiatives based on user impact and strategic value.

The primary focus was placed on reducing **posting anxiety and contribution friction**, followed by mechanisms that encourage meaningful participation and professional reputation building.

---

### 9. Success Metrics

**Objective:** Define KPIs to measure the effectiveness of the proposed product improvements.

**North Star Metric — Weekly Active Contributors (WAC)**

Percentage of WAU who create at least one qualifying contribution per week.

### LinkedIn Moments

- Adoption Rate
- New-Poster Activation
- Creator Repeat Rate
- Viewer Engagement Rate

### Community Badges

- Contribution Rate
- Activation Rate
- Contributor Retention
- Badge Earn Rate

## Key Insights

- LinkedIn engagement is strongly connected to immediate career needs.
- Usage decreases after users secure job opportunities.
- Users experience pressure to post impressive achievements.
- Achievement-heavy and self-promotional content can reduce feed value.
- AI-generated content has contributed to reduced trust in the feed.
- Users need lower-friction ways to participate professionally.
- Meaningful recognition can encourage active contribution.
- LinkedIn has an opportunity to create stronger daily engagement loops around continuous professional growth.
